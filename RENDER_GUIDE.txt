# RENDER CI/CD & CRON JOB SETUP GUIDE

This guide explains how to use the provided `render.yaml` to fully automate your SAP Research Agent on Render.

## 1. CI/CD (Continuous Deployment)
Render handles CI/CD automatically when connected to your GitHub repository.

1.  Log in to [Render Dashboard](https://dashboard.render.com).
2.  Click **"New +"** and select **"Blueprint"**.
3.  Connect your GitHub repository: `sap_research_hackathon_2026`.
4.  Render will automatically detect the `render.yaml` file.
5.  **Environment Variables**: You must add your secrets in the Render Dashboard (under "Environment" or the Blueprints setup screen):
    - `ACE_API_KEY`
    - `SAP_API_KEY`
    - `SOLANA_PRIVATE_KEY`
6.  **Auto-Deploy**: Every time you `git push`, Render will automatically build the new version and redeploy the Dashboard and API.

---

## 2. Setting Up External Cron Jobs (FREE)
Render's built-in cron jobs are a paid feature. To keep your agent running 24/7 for free, we recommend using an external service like [cron-job.org](https://cron-job.org).

### Essential Jobs to Configure:
You should set up two separate cron jobs pointing to your deployed API URL:

1.  **Keep-Alive (Avoid Sleeping)**
    - **URL**: `https://your-app-name.onrender.com/ping`
    - **Schedule**: Every 14 minutes
    - **Purpose**: Prevents Render from spinning down your web service.

2.  **Trigger Research (Activity Volume)**
    - **URL**: `https://your-app-name.onrender.com/trigger-run`
    - **Schedule**: Every hour (or as desired)
    - **Purpose**: Manually kicks off the autonomous research loop if the startup loop is idle.

### Why this is better:
- **Zero Cost**: Most external cron services have a generous free tier.
- **Reliability**: Ensures your Dashboard is always "instant-load" for judges and users.
- **Volume**: Consistently generates research logs every hour, which look great on your dashboard history.

## 3. Dashboard UX & Visualization
The new dashboard is designed to showcase the **Volume and Intensity** of your agent's work.
- **Lifecycle Visualization**: Shows exactly where the agent is in the SAP/Ace loop.
- **Raw Intelligence Feed**: A live terminal-style log that proves the agent is actively processing DePIN intelligence.
- **Reputation (AQ)**: Reflects your agent's standing in the network based on successful settlements.

🥇 **Goal**: Aim for at least 10 entries in your "Raw Intelligence Feed" before submitting!
